# 0043b. Device Management (Feature 68)

## Summary

Feature 68 gives coaching center owners self service visibility and control over their licensed PCs inside the customer portal (`/portal`). Owners can view every linked device with its custom label, hostname, OS version, app version, active student count, and last seen timestamp, rename device labels, deactivate a lost or retired PC after confirming a 6 digit step up OTP (which releases the license slot, issues a fresh 6 digit pairing code, and transitions the old PC into immediate `deactivated_read_only` mode on its next refresh via Desktop Migration 0023), or replace an old PC in one step by supplying the new PC's offline `REQ-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX` code or online pairing slot within the shared 3 transfers per 12 months policy.

## Requirements

Satisfies umbrella acceptance criteria **AC-5**, **AC-6**, **AC-7**, **AC-8**, **AC-9**, and **AC-10** in [index.md](index.md).

## Decision & Inline Rationale

We implement portal device management across `crates/cloud_api` (`installations` extended with `status = 'deactivated'`, `hostname`, `os_version`, `deactivated_at`, `deactivation_reason`, `replaced_by_installation_id`, and `device_management_events`), `crates/core` (`migrations/0023_license_deactivation_state.sql` and `LicenseMode::DeactivatedReadOnly`), and `Landing pages/` (`/portal` Device Management Studio). Separating `deactivated` (portal owner action on a lost or retired PC, 0 grace days) from `moved` (desktop side by side transfer, 14 transitional days) ensures a deactivated computer becomes read only on its very next refresh while never deleting local SQLite data or blocking exports and backups.

## Feature design

**Device Deactivation, Replacement, and Refresh Rules**:
* **Device inventory & rename (`GET /v1/portal/devices`, `POST /v1/portal/devices/rename`)**: Lists all `installations` for `session.customer_id` ordered by `status = 'active'` first then `last_seen_at DESC`, plus `transfers_used_12m = licenses.transfer_count_12m`, `transfers_remaining_12m = max(0, 3 - transfer_count_12m)`, and `device_management_events`. Renaming updates `installations.label` without consuming a transfer slot.
* **Step up OTP verification (`POST /v1/portal/devices/request_action_otp`)**: Generates a 6 digit OTP stored in `customer_portal_credentials.otp_code_hash` (10 minute expiry) required by both `deactivate` and `replace`.
* **Portal deactivation (`POST /v1/portal/devices/deactivate`)**: Verifies `transfer_count_12m < 3` and the 6 digit OTP; sets `installations.status = 'deactivated'`, `deactivated_at = now`, `deactivation_reason = reason`; clears `licenses.bound_installation_id = NULL`; increments `transfer_count_12m` and `current_serial`; generates a fresh 72 hour 6 digit `pairing_code` on `licenses`; and writes an immutable row to `device_management_events`.
* **One step portal replacement (`POST /v1/portal/devices/replace`)**: Verifies `transfer_count_12m < 3` and the 6 digit OTP; marks `old_installation_id` as `deactivated` with `replaced_by_installation_id`; increments `transfer_count_12m` and `current_serial`; and:
  * If `new_request_code_or_qr` (`REQ-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX` or `COACHIFY-REQ|v1|...`) is provided, validates the HMAC checksum, extracts `new_installation_id`, binds `licenses.bound_installation_id = new_installation_id`, upserts the new `installations` row as `active` with `new_label`, calls `crates/license_signer` to sign the new `LicenseEnvelope`, records `license_issues` and `device_management_events` (`replace_offline_req`), returns `signed_license_blob` for immediate `.coachifylicense` download, and displays a confirmation notice in `/portal` informing the owner that the new `.coachifylicense` is active immediately and the old PC will switch to read only mode the next time it connects to the internet or when its local billing period ends.
  * If no offline request code is provided (`replace_online`), clears `bound_installation_id` and returns a fresh 6 digit `pairing_code` pre labeled with `new_label` for the replacement PC's online activation bar.
* **Desktop `RefreshStatus::Deactivated` handling (`crates/core/src/licensing.rs` & Migration 0023)**: When `POST /v1/refresh` finds `installations.status == 'deactivated'` for the calling `(license_id, installation_id)`, it returns `RefreshStatus::Deactivated { deactivated_at, reason }`. The desktop refresh client writes `last_refresh_result = 'deactivated'`, `deactivated_at`, and `deactivation_reason` to `license_refresh_state` (Migration 0023). The dispatcher evaluates `deactivated_at IS NOT NULL` as `LicenseMode::DeactivatedReadOnly` immediately (no 14 day window), rejecting write commands with `LICENSE_DEVICE_DEACTIVATED` while keeping login, viewing, reports, printing, CSV/Excel exports, and backup/restore open.
* **3 transfer limit banner and support reset (`POST /v1/portal/devices/request_transfer_reset` & `admin.licenses.reset_transfer_count`)**: When `transfer_count_12m >= 3`, `/portal` shows a clear limit banner and a one click Request Support Reset button (`action = 'support_reset_request'` in `device_management_events`), which `ops_admin` or `super_admin` can reset in Vendor Admin (`admin.licenses.reset_transfer_count`).
