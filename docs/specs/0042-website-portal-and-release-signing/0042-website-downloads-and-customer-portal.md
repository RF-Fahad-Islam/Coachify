# 0042a. Website, Downloads, Privacy Transparency, and Customer Portal (Feature 65)

**Date**: 2026-09-25

## Summary

Feature 65 completes Coachify's public web presence and self service customer portal inside `Landing pages/` and `crates/cloud_api`. It pairs the story driven English and Bangla marketing website, searchable help center (`/help`), and live channel Download Center (`/downloads`) with a dedicated Telemetry and Privacy Transparency page (`/privacy`) that discloses the exact 5 fields sent by the desktop app, plus an authenticated Customer Portal (`/portal`) where institution owners sign in with phone or email, password, and a 6 digit OTP to download their `.coachifylicense` file, verified Windows installer, and `INV-YYYY-NNNNN` vendor invoices, and view their linked PC installations.

## Requirements

**User stories**:
* As a coaching center owner evaluating Coachify, I want to read the product story in English or Bangla, inspect the exact 5 desktop telemetry fields on `/privacy`, and download the verified Windows installer with its SHA256 checksum on `/downloads`.
* As a paying institution owner, I want to sign in to `/portal` with my phone number or email, password, and a 6 digit OTP code so I can download my active `.coachifylicense` file, download the current Windows installer, view and print my `INV-YYYY-NNNNN` invoices, and check my bound PC installations.

**Acceptance criteria**:
* **AC-1**: Customer portal authentication and OTP session security (`POST /v1/portal/login`, `POST /v1/portal/verify_otp`, Argon2id password hash, 6 digit OTP valid for 10 minutes, 5 attempt lockout for 15 minutes, SHA256 hashed bearer token in `customer_portal_sessions`).
* **AC-2**: Customer portal license, installer, invoice, and installation access (`GET /v1/portal/overview`, `GET /v1/portal/license_file/{license_id}`, `GET /v1/portal/invoice/{invoice_id}`, strict `customer_id` ownership check, `.coachifylicense` download, `INV-YYYY-NNNNN` invoice view, and linked PC installations table).
* **AC-3**: Exact privacy and telemetry transparency page (`/privacy` route in `Landing pages/` showing the exact 5 desktop activation/refresh JSON fields `installation_id`, `license_id`, `current_serial`, `app_version`, `active_students` and 2 update check fields, plus zero student PII guarantee).
* **AC-4**: Live download center (`/downloads`) and help center (`/help`) with `stable` and `beta` channel metadata, SHA256 checksum verification, Authenticode signer badge, and bilingual English/Bangla guides.
* **AC-9**: Visual alignment with `design.md` and `Landing pages/` (`Sora`, `Inter`, `Noto Sans Bengali`, `#faf7f2` warm cream, `#16110d` ink, `#f97316` ember orange, `WindowFrame`, `CtaButton`, `SectionTag`, and `Chip`).

## Decision

**Chosen option**: Extend `crates/cloud_api` with `customer_portal_credentials` and `customer_portal_sessions` plus versioned `/v1/portal/*` endpoints, and add `/portal` and `/privacy` routes alongside live channel metadata on `/downloads` in `Landing pages/`.

**Implementation skills**: `rust-best-practices` (`apollographql/skills`, `.claude/skills/rust-best-practices/`) · `shadcn` (`shadcn/ui`, `.claude/skills/shadcn/`) · `tailwind-4-docs` (`lombiq/tailwind-agent-skills`, `.claude/skills/tailwind-4-docs/`) · `vitest` (`antfu/skills`, `.claude/skills/vitest/`)

## Rationale

Storing customer portal credentials and sessions in `crates/cloud_api` directly beside `customers`, `subscriptions`, `licenses`, `vendor_invoices`, and `installations` gives `/portal` immediate access to every signed `.coachifylicense` blob and `INV-YYYY-NNNNN` invoice generated by Vendor Admin (Feature 63) and online activation (Feature 64) without any secondary database sync. Using phone or email plus Argon2id password and a 6 digit OTP aligns with Bangladeshi coaching center owners who primarily identify their accounts by mobile phone number.

## Feature design

**Data model sketch**:
* `customer_portal_credentials`: `customer_id TEXT PRIMARY KEY REFERENCES customers(id)`, `password_hash TEXT NOT NULL`, `otp_code_hash TEXT`, `otp_expires_at TEXT`, `failed_attempts INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0`, `locked_until TEXT`, `updated_at TEXT NOT NULL`.
* `customer_portal_sessions`: `id TEXT PRIMARY KEY`, `customer_id TEXT NOT NULL REFERENCES customers(id)`, `session_token_hash TEXT UNIQUE NOT NULL`, `ip_address TEXT`, `user_agent TEXT`, `expires_at TEXT NOT NULL`, `revoked_at TEXT`, `created_at TEXT NOT NULL`.

**State transitions**:
* `unauthenticated` -> (`POST /v1/portal/login`) -> `otp_pending` -> (`POST /v1/portal/verify_otp`) -> `authenticated` -> (`POST /v1/portal/logout` or expiry) -> `revoked`.

**API surface**:

| Endpoint | Method | Key inputs | Key outputs | Auth | Key errors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `/v1/portal/login` | `POST` | `identifier`, `password` | `customer_id`, `otp_required`, `masked_destination`, `otp_expires_at` | Public (rate limited) | `INVALID_CREDENTIALS`, `PORTAL_ACCOUNT_LOCKED` |
| `/v1/portal/verify_otp` | `POST` | `customer_id`, `otp_code` | `session_token`, `expires_at`, `customer` | Public (rate limited) | `INVALID_OTP`, `OTP_EXPIRED`, `PORTAL_ACCOUNT_LOCKED` |
| `/v1/portal/overview` | `GET` | `session_token` | `customer`, `subscription`, `license`, `invoices`, `installations`, `latest_release` | Customer Bearer token | `UNAUTHORIZED_PORTAL_SESSION`, `CUSTOMER_SUSPENDED` |
| `/v1/portal/license_file/{license_id}` | `GET` | `session_token`, `license_id` | `filename`, `signed_blob`, `serial` | Customer Bearer token | `FORBIDDEN_LICENSE_OWNER`, `LICENSE_NOT_FOUND` |
| `/v1/portal/invoice/{invoice_id}` | `GET` | `session_token`, `invoice_id` | `invoice_number`, `issued_at`, `org_name`, `plan_name`, `period_months`, `method`, `reference`, `subtotal_poisha`, `vat_poisha`, `total_poisha` | Customer Bearer token | `FORBIDDEN_INVOICE_OWNER`, `INVOICE_NOT_FOUND` |

**Value sourcing**:

| Action | Value produced / displayed | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `/portal` License Status & Download Card | `plan_code`, `status`, `paid_through`, `current_serial`, and `<license_id>.coachifylicense` file | `subscriptions`, `licenses.last_issued_blob`, and `GET /v1/portal/license_file/{license_id}` |
| `/portal` Verified Installer Card | `version`, `channel`, `sha256`, `authenticode_thumbprint`, `artifact_url` | `release_channel_heads['stable']` joined with `releases` |
| `/portal` Vendor Invoices Table | `invoice_number` (`INV-YYYY-NNNNN`), `issued_at`, `method`, `reference`, `total_poisha` | `vendor_invoices` joined with `payments` filtered by `customer_id` |
| `/portal` Linked PC Installations List | `label`, `installation_id`, `app_version`, `active_students`, `last_seen_at`, `status` | `installations` filtered by `customer_id` |
| `/privacy` Telemetry Inspector | Exact 5 activation/refresh JSON fields and zero PII guarantee | `DesktopActivateRequest` and `DesktopRefreshRequest` contract in `crates/cloud_api` |

**Key invariants**:
* Every `/v1/portal/*` query filters strictly by `customer_portal_sessions.customer_id`.
* 5 consecutive wrong OTP attempts lock `customer_portal_credentials.locked_until` for 15 minutes.

**Security model**:
* Argon2id password hash + 6 digit OTP verification + SHA256 hashed session bearer token (`customer_portal_sessions`).

**Configuration required**:
* `VITE_CLOUD_API_URL`: Base URL for commercial portal API requests (with deterministic embedded client state for offline preview and tests).

**Critical test scenarios**:
* Happy path: Customer logs in with phone + password + OTP, downloads `.coachifylicense`, views `INV-2026-00001`, and inspects bound PC installations, verifies **AC-1**, **AC-2**, **AC-9**.
* Privacy and Download verification: Visiting `/privacy` renders the exact 5 telemetry fields (`installation_id`, `license_id`, `current_serial`, `app_version`, `active_students`) and visiting `/downloads` displays `stable` and `beta` release checksums, verifies **AC-3**, **AC-4**.
* Auth/permission: Customer A requesting Customer B's `license_id` or `invoice_id` receives `FORBIDDEN_LICENSE_OWNER` / `FORBIDDEN_INVOICE_OWNER`, verifies **AC-1**, **AC-2**.

## Build plan

1. Add `customer_portal_credentials` and `customer_portal_sessions` tables and `/v1/portal/*` handlers (`portal_login`, `portal_verify_otp`, `portal_get_overview`, `portal_download_license_file`, `portal_get_invoice`) in `crates/cloud_api`, satisfies **AC-1**, **AC-2**
2. Build `/portal` (`PortalPage.tsx`) with the 4 confirmed sections (License Status & `.coachifylicense` Download, Verified Installer Download, Paid Vendor Invoices `INV-YYYY-NNNNN`, Linked PC Installations), `/privacy` (`PrivacyPage.tsx`) with the 5 field JSON telemetry inspector, and live channel release metadata on `/downloads` in `Landing pages/`, satisfies **AC-1**, **AC-2**, **AC-3**, **AC-4**, **AC-9**

## Consequences

**Positive**:
* Customers can download their `.coachifylicense` file, installer, and `INV-YYYY-NNNNN` invoices any time without waiting on human support.
* Prospective customers can audit the exact 5 fields sent by the desktop app on `/privacy`.

**Negative / tradeoffs**:
* Requires coaching center owners to complete a 6 digit OTP check on login once every 24 hours.
